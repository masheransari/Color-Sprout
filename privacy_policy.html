Privacy Policy

App: Color Sprout
Effective date: October 12, 2026
Contact: asheransar@gmail.com

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Color Sprout, a coloring app with coloring pages, drawing tools, color selection, undo and redo, and saved artwork.

Summary: Artwork and app preferences are used to provide the coloring experience. When you choose to export, share, or contact support, the destination application or service handles the information you send under its own privacy policy.

1. Artwork and App Preferences

Color Sprout processes the coloring pages you select, your drawing and coloring actions, and the artwork you save. Preferences such as tool selections and left-handed layout settings support your experience. Saved artwork and preferences can remain in app storage until deleted or cleared.

2. Saving and Exporting

When you save or export an image to a location outside app storage, that copy is managed by your device or the destination service. Deleting artwork inside Color Sprout does not necessarily delete exported copies. Device backup or cloud synchronization settings may also affect copies of saved information.

3. Sharing

If you choose to share artwork or an app link, the receiving app or service processes the content you send. You control the destination. Its privacy policy governs its handling of that information.

4. Contacting Support

If you contact asheransar@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you provide. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Avoid including personal information in screenshots or artwork unless necessary for your request.

5. Permissions and Device Services

Device services may be used to save or share files and open an email application. Where Android requires a permission for an action, access is controlled through Android’s permission settings. Revoking access may prevent the associated action from completing.

6. External Services

External email, sharing, storage, and backup services operate under their own privacy policies. If you follow a link to another service, that service may receive connection information, including your IP address.

7. Children’s Privacy

Color Sprout’s coloring activities may be used by children. Parents and guardians should supervise sharing and support requests, which can disclose information to external services. We do not ask users to put their name, address, or other personal information into their artwork. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required by applicable law.

8. Retention and Deletion

App-stored artwork and preferences can be removed by clearing Color Sprout’s storage through Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes app-local storage, but exported files and backup copies may remain. Delete those copies through the application or service that holds them.

Support correspondence is retained to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. Request access, correction, or deletion of information sent to support by emailing the contact address above. Information required for legal obligations may need to be retained.

9. Security

Device security, access controls, and the services you choose affect the protection of your artwork and correspondence. No storage system or internet transmission is completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage permissions, clear app storage, control device backups, and decide whether to export or share artwork. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights regarding personal information held by us. Contact us to exercise those rights.

11. International Processing

Email and other external services may process information in countries outside your own, according to their policies and applicable law.

12. Changes and Contact

Updates will be published here with a revised effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to asheransar@gmail.com.
